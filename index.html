Common Challenges in Product-Based Businesses

These are challenges many inventory-led businesses encounter. Not every business faces all of them.

Manual inventory tracking

Stock counts kept in spreadsheets or on paper can fall out of date quickly.

Disconnected business applications

Store, accounting, sales and reporting tools often run separately.

Difficulty managing orders and stock

Without one view, it is harder to know what is available, reserved or on order.

Repetitive administrative work

Teams spend time on routine updates, reminders and document preparation.

Limited visibility into performance

Reports take effort to compile, so decisions rely on partial or late data.

Manual data entry between systems

Re-keying the same details raises the chance of errors and delays.

Inventory & Order Management

Zoho Inventory Setup and Configuration

Zoho Inventory helps product-based businesses track stock, manage sales and purchase orders, and keep item data organised. We configure it around how your team actually receives, stores and ships goods.

  • Item master, units, variants, price lists and bundles
  • Multi-warehouse stock tracking and transfers
  • Batch, serial number and expiry tracking where your products need it
  • Sales order, purchase order and shipment workflows
  • Reorder levels and stock-aging visibility

Challenge it may address: Manual stock tracking and limited stock visibility.
Our support: Configuration of items, warehouses and order workflows, plus migration of item and stock data.

How Your Business Flows Through Zoho

A connected workflow from first enquiry to final report. Click any step to see what happens there.

  1. Enquiry & quote

    Leads and enquiries are captured in Zoho CRM, followed up by your sales team, and turned into quotes.

  2. Order received

    Orders arrive from your Zoho Commerce store, existing store or sales team and become sales orders.

  3. Stock & fulfilment

    Zoho Inventory confirms availability, reserves stock, and manages packing and shipment.

  4. Invoice & payment

    Zoho Books raises the invoice, records payments and keeps accounting in step with stock.

  5. Reporting

    Zoho Analytics shows sales, stock and receivables in dashboards your management can review.

  1. Reorder review

    Stock levels and reorder points in Zoho Inventory show what needs to be purchased.

  2. Purchase order

    Purchase orders are created for vendors and tracked until goods arrive.

  3. Goods received

    Receipts update stock by warehouse, with batch or serial details where required.

  4. Bill & payment

    Zoho Books records the vendor bill and payment against the purchase.

  5. Spend & stock reports

    Zoho Analytics summarises purchases, payables and stock position.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Zoho applications can Zolytics help implement?

Our services cover Zoho Inventory, Zoho Books, Zoho CRM, Zoho Analytics, Zoho Creator and integrations between Zoho and other systems. The right combination depends on your requirements.

Can you help businesses migrate from spreadsheets or existing software?

Yes, data migration is part of our services. What can be migrated, and how, is confirmed during discovery once we see your current data and systems.

Can Zoho applications integrate with our existing systems?

Often, through Zoho Flow, APIs or custom functions, but it depends on the system. We check feasibility for each integration before agreeing scope.

Do you work with e-commerce and distribution businesses?

Yes. Our focus is product-based businesses such as online retailers, wholesalers, distributors, importers and exporters, and hardware or industrial suppliers.

How does a Zoho implementation project begin?

With a discovery discussion about your current processes and goals. We then plan a suitable solution and agree the scope before configuration starts.

What information is needed before requesting a consultation?

Your business type, the Zoho apps or processes you are interested in, and a short description of your current workflow. Details are optional if you are still exploring.
